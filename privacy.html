Privacy Policy

PM Second Brain is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's Google Calendar (read-only) and reads and updates the owner's Google Tasks. This data is processed locally on the owner's own computer and stored only in the owner's private notes.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
